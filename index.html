SHEET A-01Firm profile

One contractor, accountable for the whole job.

Most renovations go sideways in the gaps — between the designer and the framer, the plumber and the tiler. Northview works design-build: one team owns your project from first sketch to final walkthrough, so nothing gets lost between trades.

We're a Vaughan-based crew of carpenters, project managers and finishers. No hand-offs, no finger-pointing — just a priced scope, a real schedule, and a site kept clean enough that your neighbours won't hate you.

Every estimate is itemized line by line. If a number moves, you'll know why before it does.

SHEET A-04Project phases

From first call to final walkthrough.

01
Phase 01 — Measure

Site visit & survey

We walk the property, take measurements, photos and notes, and talk through what you actually want — not what a brochure says you should want. Free, about an hour.

02
Phase 02 — Draft

Itemized proposal

Within a week you get a line-by-line quote: labour, materials, allowances, timeline. No lump-sum mysteries, no "allowance" traps.

03
Phase 03 — Build

Construction, managed

Permits pulled, site protected, schedule posted. You get a single point of contact and photo updates without having to ask.

04
Phase 04 — Sign-off

Walkthrough & warranty

We punch-list every room together before final payment. Then a 2-year workmanship warranty in writing — because that's how long we expect it to take before you even think about calling us back.
