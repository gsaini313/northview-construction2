The Firm

One contractor, accountable for everything.

Most renovations go sideways in the gaps — between the designer and the framer, the plumber and the tiler. Northview works design-build: a single team owns your project from the first sketch to the final walkthrough, so nothing gets lost between trades.

We're a Vaughan-based crew of carpenters, project managers and finishers. No hand-offs, no finger-pointing — just an itemized scope, a real schedule, and a site kept clean enough that your neighbours won't hate you.

If a number moves mid-project, you'll know why before it does. That's the whole philosophy, and it's written into every contract.

— The Northview Crew

Process

From first call to final walkthrough.

i.

Site Visit

We walk the property, take measurements and photos, and talk through what you actually want. Free, about an hour.

ii.

Itemized Proposal

Within a week: a line-by-line quote — labour, materials, allowances, timeline. No lump-sum mysteries.

iii.

Construction

Permits pulled, site protected, schedule posted. One point of contact, photo updates without having to ask.

iv.

Walkthrough

We punch-list every room together before final payment — then a 2-year workmanship warranty in writing.
